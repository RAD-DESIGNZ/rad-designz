The name traces back to Radji, short for Ridjimiraril — a character in Rolf de Heer's *Ten Canoes*, the first feature film shot entirely in Australian Aboriginal languages. It is a story told inside another story, where how the tale is told carries as much weight as what happens in it. That is close to how we think about brands.

Since 2017 we have built identities, packaging, campaigns and product interfaces for Samsung, STA Travel, Arable Protocol, Menspire and many other brands, alongside startups, sports organisations and independent businesses.

We also produce AI-assisted film under RAD SEQUENCE FILMS. Our short *The Ash Witness* was an official selection at the Runway AI Film Festival 2026.

Based in Germany. Working in English and Romanian.
