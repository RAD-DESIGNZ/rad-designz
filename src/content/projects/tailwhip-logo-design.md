  Logo Design for Tailwhip, a custom bike builder in Cluj-Napoca
  — logo, pattern and merch.

  Tailwhip builds custom bikes in Cluj-Napoca. The identity had to
  look at home on a frame, a shirt and a workshop wall.

  The wordmark is handwritten and leans forward, the way a rider
  does. Under it, three chevrons stack into a downhill arrow — a
  tyre tread read from above, and the shape a tailwhip leaves in the
  air. One green, flat black, no gradients: it prints on a sticker,
  cuts in vinyl, and survives a frame decal.

  The pattern repeats that mark until it becomes texture — tread
  across a surface rather than a logo pasted on it.
