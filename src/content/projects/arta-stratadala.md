  We developed a bold visual identity for Arta Stradala, a creative project rooted in street culture, urban expression and the energy of public spaces.

  The identity combines a geometric symbol built around an abstract A, paired with a vibrant palette. We kept the system simple, raw and flexible — designed to work as naturally on posters and murals as it does across digital platforms.

  Alongside the core brand identity, we created a custom icon set and graphic pattern system, giving Arta Stradala a distinctive visual language that can expand across different applications while keeping the brand instantly recognizable.
